# How to play Wordbright

**Goal:** make words on the board and score the most points.

1. **Your tiles.** Everyone has 7 letter tiles. Each tile shows its points (e = 1, q = 7).
2. **First word.** It must cover the start square ● (on the Large board: at least one of the four start squares in the middle). It can go across or down.
3. **Taking a turn.** Put 1 or more tiles in one straight line, across or down, with no gaps. Every new word must join a word already on the board. Every word you make (the long one and any short ones across it) must be a real word.
4. **Points.** Add up the points on the tiles in each word you made. Bonus squares count only on the turn a tile is first placed on them:

   | Square | What it does |
   |---|---|
   | **Spark** (2L) | that letter × 2 |
   | **Glow** (3L) | that letter × 3 |
   | **Shine** (2W) | the whole word × 2 |
   | **Star** (3W) | the whole word × 3 |

   Two word-squares in one word multiply together (two Shines = × 4).
5. **Star words.** If you make a word from the Year 3–6 spelling list, you get **+5** for each one, and you see what it means.
6. **Full-rack bonus.** Use all 7 tiles in one turn: **+30**.
7. **Blank tiles.** A blank can be any letter. It scores 0.
8. **Swap or pass.** Instead of playing, you can swap some tiles with the bag (you miss that turn), or pass.
9. **New tiles.** After your turn, take tiles from the bag until you have 7 again.
10. **End of the game.** The game ends when the bag is empty and one player has used all their tiles, or when everyone passes three times in a row. Each player takes away the points of the tiles still in their rack. Highest score wins.
