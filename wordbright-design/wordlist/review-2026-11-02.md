# Word-list review, 2 Nov 2026 (G7b): slurs the child-safe filter missed

G7 found "kraut", "krauts", "jewed" and "jewing" in the full, large and xlarge lists, and
the computer played two of them. This review looked for that whole class of words in every list.

## Why they got past the filter
- **WordNet check, case bug.** `tools/build-meanings.py --prune-lists` blocks a word when every
  WordNet sense is marked offensive, but it counted a sense only if the lower-case word was a
  member of the synset. WordNet writes most slurs with a capital ("Kraut", "Boche", "Jerry",
  "Mick"), so these words had no senses at all and were never blocked. Fixed: the match ignores case.
- **No WordNet entry.** Verb slurs made from a group's name ("jewed", "welsher") are not in
  WordNet, and neither the open blocklist (LDNOOBW) nor our own list had them. Found by hand.
- **Our own list was short** on ethnic, disability, sexuality and gender slurs and their forms.

## How the candidates were found
1. WordNet 3.1: every sense with a usage domain of ethnic_slur, disparagement, obscenity, slang
   or vulgarism (the only offensive-type domains in WordNet 3.1; "profanity" and the others in the
   code are not used), and every sense whose gloss says offensive, derogatory, disparaging,
   contemptuous, slur, insult, vulgar or obscene. 1,198 list words matched, most of them harmless
   (e.g. "contemptible", "corpse"); each match was read.
2. Our own sweep: several hundred ethnic, religious, racial, disability, sexuality and gender slurs and
   insults, each with its plurals, verb forms (-s, -ed, -ing), -er, -y, -ish and -ism forms,
   checked against all five lists (starter to xlarge).

## Rules used
- Every sense a slur, insult or obscenity: blocked.
- An innocent main meaning as well: blocked when the innocent meaning is rare; in doubt, blocked.
- A Star word, or a word in the starter or steady list: not blocked by the review; listed under "Ron decides".
  Ron decided all 47 on 2 Nov 2026: 39 blocked, 8 kept (source "Ron, 2 Nov" below).

## Result
- Blocked: 197 words: 158 from the review and 39 decided by Ron on 2 Nov (all from the
  starter or steady list: 5 starter, 34 steady). All are in `OWN_BLOCK` in `tools/build_lists.py`;
  Ron's 39 are also in `RON_APPROVED_STARTER_STEADY`, the only starter/steady words
  `tools/build-meanings.py --prune-lists` may remove (any other match there is only reported).
  They are gone from every list, from `words_all.dawg` and from the meanings shards.
- Ron decides: none left (all 47 decided on 2 Nov).
- Checked and kept: 134 words (8 of them kept by Ron on 2 Nov).
- Letter values and tile bags: unchanged (`tiles.json` is not rebuilt by this step).
- In meanings, the names of peoples ("Jews", "Mongols", "Huns", "Welsh") may still appear,
  although the lower-case game words are blocked.
