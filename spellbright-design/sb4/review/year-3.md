## Chapter 6 — Un-, dis- and mis-  (y3-n01)

Focus: prefixes un-, dis-, mis-

Adding un-, dis- or mis- to the start of a word changes its meaning, and the rest of the word stays the same, as in our examples.

## Chapter 8 — Adding -ly  (y3-n03)

Focus: -ly, including -ily and -ally

Adding -ly turns a describing word into one that says how something is done; words ending in y change to -ily and words ending in -ic add -ally, as in our examples.
